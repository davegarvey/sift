Read at your own pace.

Sift is a small RSS reader for following the sites you choose, without a ranking feed or a required account.

Your library stays with you

Your subscriptions, reading state and saved articles live in your browser. You can export and import subscriptions with OPML. Optional sync shares subscriptions and reading state across your devices; you can delete that server-side data from Settings.

Open source and self-hostable

Sift is open source under the MIT License. You can run your own instance and keep its sync and polling data on storage you control.
